PBQL: Pickleball Query Language

PBQL is a SQL-like language for querying shots in pickleball games analyzed by PB Vision. A query names the videos or folders to search, filters shots by their properties, attaches surrounding context, and orders/limits the results: 

FROM "83gyqyc10y8f"
WHERE shot.type = "drop" AND shot.hitter = me AND shot.quality.overall >= 0.8
CONTEXT BEFORE 1 shot
CONTEXT AFTER 2secs
ORDER BY shot.speed DESC
LIMIT 50

Selected shots can drive the Shot Explorer in the PB Vision web app, be exported as JSON/CSV, or be turned into an EDL or ffmpeg command that cuts the clips into a reel.

Sample Queries

Show me all my drop shots:

FROM "83gyqyc10y8f"
WHERE shot.type = "drop" AND shot.hitter = me

My 20 highest-quality detected shots, best first:

FROM "83gyqyc10y8f"
WHERE shot.hitter = me AND exists(shot.quality.overall)
ORDER BY shot.quality.overall DESC
LIMIT 20

Anna's winners, with the shot before each one for context:

FROM "83gyqyc10y8f"
WHERE shot.taggedWith("Anna*") AND exists(shot.winnerType)
CONTEXT BEFORE 1 shot

Every shot of every rally I won, whole rallies as clips:

FROM "83gyqyc10y8f"
WHERE rally.winner = me.team
CONTEXT BEFORE rally
CONTEXT AFTER rally

My average shot speed by shot type:

SELECT shot.type, avg(shot.speed) AS "average speed (mph)"
FROM "83gyqyc10y8f"
WHERE shot.hitter = me AND exists(shot.type)
GROUP BY shot.type

How often we win the rally when I drive the third shot vs drop it (the third shot is one row per rally, so rallies are counted once):

SELECT shot.type, avg(rally.winner = me.team) AS "win rate", count()
FROM "83gyqyc10y8f"
WHERE shot.hitter = me AND shot.num = 3 AND shot.type IN ("drive", "drop")
GROUP BY shot.type
